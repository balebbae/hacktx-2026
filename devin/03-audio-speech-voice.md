## Goal
Everything the wearer hears and everything the app hears: one audio engine owner with two routes, a transcriber with a rolling 90 s window, a wake word plus command grammar, ElevenLabs text-to-speech with a disk cache and an Apple fallback, the cue policy and gate, and the roll call. The Simulator has no SpeechAnalyzer and no Bluetooth: make every piece testable with injected audio buffers and `FakeTranscriber`, and keep the device-only code paths small and obvious.

## Audio
- **`AudioEngineController`** (single owner, `final class`, serial queue). Configures `AVAudioSession` **once at launch and never deactivates it** (re-activation causes Bluetooth setup lag and clipped first syllables).
  - Route A `.phoneMicGlassesA2DP` (default): category `.playAndRecord`, mode `.default` (**not** `.voiceChat`, which implies HFP), options `[.allowBluetoothA2DP, .defaultToSpeaker]`, `setPreferredInput` to the built-in mic. This keeps the HFP route closed, which the glasses SDK requires while streaming.
  - Route B `.glassesHFP`: `.playAndRecord`, mode `.voiceChat`, options `[.allowBluetoothHFP, .allowBluetoothA2DP]`, `setPreferredInput` to the Bluetooth HFP port, `inputNode.setVoiceProcessingEnabled(true)` before `prepare()` (real echo cancellation; expect format changes).
  - `.phoneOnly`: no Bluetooth options. The route is injectable and switchable from Settings; switching tears down and rebuilds the tap. Expose `var glassesStreamActive: Bool` that makes Route B refuse to engage (S6 sets it).
- One `AVAudioEngine`. `inputNode.installTap(onBus: 0, bufferSize: 4096, format: inputNode.outputFormat(forBus: 0))` → `AVAudioConverter` (`primeMethod = .none`) → 16 kHz mono Float32 `AudioChunk`s with host-clock timestamps (`AVAudioTime.seconds(forHostTime:)`) → `EventBus` fan-out exposed as `EngineAudioInput: AudioInput`. Observe `AVAudioEngineConfigurationChange` and `AVAudioSession.routeChangeNotification`: re-install the tap with the new input format and restart the engine; publish `micLevel` (RMS at 10 Hz) for the judge view.
- TTS playback through an `AVAudioPlayerNode` on the same engine (16 kHz mono converted to the engine's output format). `speak` returns only after playback completes, with 150 ms of leading silence per utterance. While speaking (and 300 ms after), the samples delivered to the **Transcriber's subscription** are zeroed; the VAD and the ring buffer receive raw audio. For `audience == .otherPerson`, call `overrideOutputAudioPort(.speaker)` for the utterance and reset to `.none` afterwards.
- **`RingBuffer`:** 30 s of 16 kHz Float32 with host timestamps; `slice(from:to:) -> [Float]`; `writeWAV(url:)` as 16-bit PCM.
- **`LoudnessSplit: VoiceActivity`:** energy VAD (20 ms frames, adaptive noise floor + 8 dB, 250 ms hangover) and speaker guess: `calibrateUser(seconds:)` records RMS while the wearer reads a sentence → `userRMS`; speech above 0.6 × userRMS is `.user`, otherwise `.other`. When the iOS 26 transcriber runs, also consume `SpeechDetector` results if available and prefer them for `isSpeech`.

## Speech
- **`SpeechAnalyzerTranscriber: Transcriber`** (iOS 26, behind `#available`): `let locale = await SpeechTranscriber.supportedLocale(equivalentTo: .current) ?? Locale(identifier: "en-US")`; `SpeechTranscriber(locale: locale, preset: .timeIndexedProgressiveTranscription)` with `.transcriptionConfidence` added to the attribute options; ensure assets with `AssetInventory.assetInstallationRequest(supporting: [transcriber])?.downloadAndInstall()` (expose progress to the UI); `SpeechAnalyzer(modules: [SpeechDetector(detectionOptions: .init(sensitivityLevel: .medium), reportResults: false), transcriber])` — if `SpeechDetector` does not compile as a module on this SDK, omit it and say so. Audio: `let fmt = await SpeechAnalyzer.bestAvailableAudioFormat(compatibleWith: [transcriber], considering: inputFormat)`; convert each `AudioChunk` to an `AVAudioPCMBuffer` in `fmt` (a format mismatch produces silence, not an error); wrap as `AnalyzerInput(buffer:)` into an `AsyncStream`; `try await analyzer.start(inputSequence:)`; consume `transcriber.results`: volatile results update a `volatileText` property used by the wake word; `isFinal` results become `TranscriptSegment`s (start / end from `result.range` mapped to host time; `speaker` from `LoudnessSplit` at that time). Keep a 90 s window. Stop with `finalizeAndFinishThroughEndOfInput()`. Fallback **`SFSpeechTranscriber`** using `SFSpeechRecognizer` with `requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true`, restarted every 50 s (iOS 18, or when `SpeechTranscriber.isAvailable` is false); it is the only path that needs `SFSpeechRecognizer.requestAuthorization`.
- **`WakeWord`:** watches volatile text for a token within Levenshtein distance 2 of "familiar" (also accept "familia", "the miller", "familiar's"); after a hit, collects up to 4 s of following text (volatile + finals) and hands it to `CommandParser`.
- **`CommandParser`** (pure): regex grammar → `Command`: who's here / who is here / who's around; remember them | him | her / remember this person; quiet | normal | chatty mode; mute; unmute; call X; text | message X; where is X / where's X; cancel; say that again / repeat. Unknown → nil (the router says "Sorry, I didn't get that."). A `CommandSource` implementation publishes parsed commands.
- **`GoodbyeDetector`:** final segments containing "nice meeting you", "nice to meet you", "see you", "take care", "catch you later" → publish a `goodbyeDetected(t:)` signal (declare the type in `Speech/SpeechContracts+.swift`).

## Voice
- **`ElevenLabsTTS`:** `POST https://api.elevenlabs.io/v1/text-to-speech/{voice_id}?output_format=pcm_16000`, headers `xi-api-key`, `Content-Type: application/json`, `Accept: audio/pcm`; body `{"text": …, "model_id": "eleven_flash_v2_5", "voice_settings": {"stability": 0.5, "similarity_boost": 0.75, "speed": 1.0}, "previous_text": "Here is what I see."}` → raw 16-bit little-endian mono 16 kHz. **`PhraseCache`:** `Caches/tts/<sha256(voiceID|model|text)>.pcm`; hit → play at once; miss → fetch, cache, play. If no first byte arrives within 1.2 s, or on any error, fall back to **`AppleTTS`**. At most 2 concurrent requests. `AppleTTS`: `AVSpeechSynthesizer.write(_:toBufferCallback:)` rendering to PCM buffers so it plays through the same engine and reports a window; voice = the best enhanced or premium `en-US` voice available.
- **`Earcon`:** a 120 ms two-tone chime (880 → 1320 Hz sine, 10 ms fades) generated once, played before utterances with `earcon == true`.
- **`Speaker` implementation:** priority queue (priority 0 interrupts and clears the queue; 1–3 FIFO; depth 2; an utterance with the same `dedupeKey` replaces the queued one), ≥ 4 s spacing between utterances of priority ≥ 1, `prewarm(_:)` synthesizes a list into the cache with concurrency 2, `isSpeaking`, `stopAll()`.
- **`CuePolicy`** (pure, injectable clock) implementing AGENTS.md: appeared (cooldown 30 s per person), approaching (20 s; dropped if arrived within 5 s), arrived (30 s), left (only if the person was close within the last 2 min), unknown close only in chatty mode (20 s), speaking (once per track-session), mode gating quiet / normal / chatty. **`CueGate`** as declared in the contracts. **`RollCall.phrase(tracks:)`** per the vocabulary (known tracks sorted by bearing degrees ascending; counts for the rest).
- **`Phrasebook.prewarmSet(names:)`** builder (in `Voice/VoiceContracts+.swift`, since `Core` is frozen): every name × 5 bands × 3 distances + approaching variants + arrived + left + all fixed phrases.

## Definition of done for a human (5 minutes, on device)
Glasses connected on Route A: a cue plays through the glasses with no clipped first syllable; take the glasses off and put them back → the mic meter keeps moving and "Familiar, who's here" still triggers; the live transcript never contains an app utterance; a consent question (`audience: .otherPerson`) comes out of the phone speaker.
